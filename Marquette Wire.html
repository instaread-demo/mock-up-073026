Burries shines as Bucks wrap up Summer League, but what’s next?

Lukas Schulze, Sports Reporter • July 23, 2026
Categories:
  • Sports
  • Sports

Five seconds remain in a tie game. Milwaukee Bucks guard Brayden Burries receives the ball on the inbound with the scarce time ticking away. After he crosses half-court, he picks up speed, down to three seconds. Burries drives in with two seconds, takes the contact, lays it up and IN.  

With just 1.5 seconds left on the clock, the Bucks took a 96-94 lead over the Philadelphia 76ers, capped off by a Brayden Burries game-winning layup. The Bucks guard capped off another outstanding performance, finishing with 27 points and five assists. 

The Summer League finale concluded a dominant four games from Burries. The rookie averaged 23.5 points per game to go along with 4.2 assists in Las Vegas. Burries also contributed 20 or more points in three of his four games, proving he can be an elite scoring presence for this upcoming season. 

Although the Bucks finished with a 2-3 record in Vegas, Summer League’s primary focus is the development of young players and to see how new draft picks fare playing NBA level basketball. 

The Bucks’ other lottery pick, Nate Ament, averaged 11.2 points per game on 38.9% from three-point range in his five Summer League appearances. A far cry from the 33.3% that he shot last season with Tennessee.  

Ament showed stark improvement throughout his time in Vegas. After failing to score more than seven points in his first two games, Ament averaged 14.3 points per game in the last three games, including a 23-point explosion against the Hornets that also saw him grab six rebounds.  

Former Marquette star Kam Jones made three appearances for the Bucks in Vegas as well, after he signed with Milwaukee on a two-way contract. The second-year guard averaged 10.0 points per game along with 5.0 assists. Jones will look to provide bench depth to the Bucks but will spend majority of his time with Bucks G-league affiliate, Wisconsin Herd.   

The Bucks now look ahead to the regular season, without their franchise superstar Giannis Antetokounmpo. This will be the first season Antetokounmpo will not be on the Bucks roster since 2013. The former Bucks star will spend the foreseeable future with the Miami Heat after the Bucks dealt him to South Beach on June 22.  

The silver lining for the Bucks is the compensation they got for Antetokounmpo. They received three players: Tyler Herro, Jaime Jaquez Jr., and Kel’el Ware. The Heat also sent over the thirteenth overall pick which became Nate Ament. 

 

The Bucks have added on to already very strong young core that includes Ryan Rollins (17.3 PPG) and Ousmane Dieng (11.0 PPG).  

The Bucks will have to go through growing pains that will make the team hard to watch at points throughout the season, but it’s now clear that the team has a sense of direction. The hiring of former Bucks assistant and accomplished Grizzlies head coach Taylor Jenkins adds to that sense of direction. 

If the Bucks play their cards right and focus on developing their young players, it might not be long before the Fiserv Forum becomes the ruckus playoff atmosphere that was just a few short years ago.
