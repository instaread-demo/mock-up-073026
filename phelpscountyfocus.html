It’s the middle of summer in the Missouri Ozarks, so a heat advisory is in effect today through 7 p.m. Tuesday.

Yes, today, Saturday, through Tuesday evening.

Daily heat index values will be around 100 to 110 degrees. Now settle down, that’s heat index values, not temperatures. More about that in just a bit.

High temperatures will be in the middle 90s to around 100. Minimal overnight relieve is expected with temperatures in the upper 70s to near 80.

Right now in the middle of Saturday afternoon it’s 87.6 degrees on the front porch, according to the fancy-pants colorful digital weather station with a sensor on the shaded front porch.

On the back porch, where there’s no shade right this minute, the good old mercury thermometer shows it to be 91 degrees.

More official temperature from the National Weather Service weather station at the Rolla National Airport up in Vichy shows the temperature at 1:53 p.m. was 88 degrees. The heat index was 99 degrees, and we’re fixing to get into a little more discussion of heat indexes here in a minute if you’ll keep on reading.

The NWS forecast for the Rolla area showed that the high would be near 89 with heat index values as high as 102, and that seems to have been accurate.

For tonight, the weather prophets predict it to be clear, then becoming mostly cloudy toward daybreak, with a low around 73. Light and variable wind.

Sunday is going to be mostly sunny and hot with a high near 95 and heat index values as high as 106. Light and variable wind becoming west around 5 mph in the afternoon.

Sunday night: Mostly clear, with a low around 75. Calm wind becoming southwest around 6 mph after midnight.

Monday: Sunny and hot, with a high near 95. Southwest wind 6 to 13 mph.

Monday night: Mostly clear, with a low around 76.

Tuesday: Sunny and hot, with a high near 93.

Tuesday night: Partly cloudy, with a low around 71.

Wednesday: Mostly sunny, with a high near 89.

Wednesday night: Mostly clear, with a low around 68.

Thursday: Mostly sunny, with a high near 88.

Thursday night: Partly cloudy, with a low around 68.

Friday: Mostly sunny, with a high near 87.

As hot as it is, you need to keep an eye on the kids if they’re outside playing. And also take some care with your fur babies.

Don’t take them outside onto pavement or sidewalk. You’d be dancing and hollering if you walked on pavement or concrete in bare feet. It is no more comfortable for their paws. Go take a look at the graphics someone sent The Weather Insider about taking care of the fur babies. The graphics are from the American Society for the Protection of Animals.

Now, what about the heat index? Click on the images included with today’s weather and you’ll find a slide to help you understand the difference between temperature and heat index. Heat index takes into account the relative humidity. Mama and Grandma understood that way back in the previous century. After a day of gardening and canning, they’d sit on the front porch, fan themselves and one of them or both would say something like. “It’s hot! What’s that thermometer say? Then The Weather Insider, just a kid then,would jump up and look and read out the number. Then one or both of them would say, “Whew. It’s not the heat but the humidity that makes it feel so hot.”

Now, people not as old as The Weather Insider and the scientists in Phelps County will say this is evidence of climate change. Maybe so. The Weather Insider is not very scientific and just figures it’s hot because it’s July, headed into August.

Whatever the reason, folks, just be careful out there the rest of the weekend and into next week. It is hot, dang hot.
