Cooke SP3 & AP3 cinema lenses now available also for Nikon Z-mount

By [NR] admin | Published: July 28, 2026
FacebookTwitterEmailShare
Listen to audio version of this article
Sponsored by
0:00 / 1:34
Instaread



Cooke Optics announced today that its Cooke SP3 and Cooke AP3 cinema lenses will also be available for Nikon Z-mount:

The Cooke user-interchangeable mount system allows filmmakers to easily switch to Nikon Z-mount. Eligible new SP3 and AP3 purchases include a complimentary second mount, with Nikon Z-mount now available as an option.

Additional information:

  • Cooke SP3 Full Frame Cine Lens Line
  • Cooke SP3 6-Lens Set
  • Cooke Anamorphic Lenses


Here are the basic Cooke AP3 specs:

  • Focal length range: 35mm / 50mm / 85mm
  • Format: Full Frame (S35 compatible)
  • Squeeze factor: 1.5x
  • Mount: Interchangeable: E (fitted); RF, L, Z & M (available to order as a free accessory)
  • Speed: T2.4
  • Form: Small and lightweight yet finished in tough anodized shell
  • Scale: Dual Focus scales


Cooke SP3 are spherical primes. Cooke AP3 are 1.5x anamorphic primes. Here is a quick comparison:

Feature Cooke SP3 Cooke AP3
Type Spherical (normal) 1.5x Anamorphic
Focal lengths 18, 25, 32, 50, 75, 100mm 35, 50, 85mm
Aperture T2.4 T2.4
Coverage Full Frame (S35 compatible) Full Frame (S35 compatible)
Weight 500-690 g 1.2-1.4 kg
Front diameter Mostly 64mm (some 82mm) 87mm
Filter thread M58 or M77 M82
Mounts Interchangeable Interchangeable
Price (approx.) $4,500 per lens $7,500–7,750 per lens
Optical Character
  • Classic “Cooke Look” based on the Speed Panchro design, re-engineered for modern full-frame mirrorless cameras.
  • Sharp on axis with gentle fall-off toward the edges.
  • Smooth, round bokeh.
  • Organic but controlled flare behavior.
  • Designed to color-match and be used alongside the higher-end Panchro/i Classic series.
  • Ground-up 1.5x anamorphic design.
  • Delivers classic anamorphic traits (oval bokeh, horizontal flares, subtle distortion) but in a more controlled, “natural” way rather than extreme vintage anamorphic.
  • Strong central sharpness, smooth defocus, and painterly bokeh.
  • Cooke positions them optically between the spherical SP3 and their high-end Anamorphic/i FF 1.8x lenses.
  • Shares coatings and mechanical architecture with the SP3, so the two series can be mixed creatively on the same production with good color consistency

Cooke Optics will host an event to test the new Z-mount at Cooke London on August 12.
