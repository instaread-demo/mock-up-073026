    Greensboro hosts its annual Fun Fourth Festival

    Josh Brown, Staff Photographer
    July 18, 2026
    Josh Brown
    A kid enjoys the water from a fire truck during the 2026 Fun Fourth Fest.
    Listen to audio version of this article

    Greensboro, NC hosted its annual Fun Fourth Fest on July 4th. Many attendees experienced stations that housed acrobats, music artists, and even a cooling station sponsored by the fire department. Afterwards, fans stuck around for a fireworks show showcased by the Greensboro Grasshoppers baseball team. Check out some of those images below.
