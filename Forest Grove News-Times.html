Oregon softball’s top assistant hired by SEC power

Published 5:37 pm Monday, July 27, 2026

By Isaac Streeter

Elon Butler (0) celebrates scoring a run for the Ducks during the 2026 softball season at Jane Sanders Stadium in Eugene. (Courtesy photo: GoDucks.com)

Sam Marder hired by Oklahoma as Sooners’ Director of Player Development

Oregon softball head coach Melyssa Lombardi lost a key component of her staff to the SEC on Thursday, July 23.

Sam Marder, the Ducks associate head coach, traded green and yellow in Eugene for the University of Oklahoma’s crimson and cream. She joins the Sooners staff as the director of player of development after five seasons on staff with the Ducks, including Oregon’s 2025 trip to the Women’s College World Series.

Marder was the 2025 Easton/National Fast-pitch Coaches Association Assistant Coach of the Year and first-ever Oregon assistant to earn the award. She helped the Ducks set numerous offensive records during her time in Eugene. In all five years she was on staff, Oregon posted top-10 seasons in program history for slugging percentage, along with three of the school’s top home run and batting average seasons.

Marder’s hiring at Oklahoma is a return to the Sooners, where she helped the squad win a national championship in 2021. She’s also coached at Ohio State (her alma mater), Boise State, the College of Charleston and Texas Tech.

At Ohio State, Marder was a three-time All-American and owns the program’s records for home runs (61), RBIs (191) and walks (232). She was inducted into the Buckeyes Hall of Fame in 2016.

She’s the second Marder hired away from Oregon by the SEC this offseason. Marder’s brother, Jack Marder, was hired by the Texas baseball team last month, where he’ll serve as the Longhorns’ director of recruiting.
