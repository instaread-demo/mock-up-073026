VSP ask for assistance in identifying vehicle following illegal dumping of 40 tires in Willoughby Forest

Published 1 week ago Tracy Davis Pierce1w ago • Bookmarks: 206

Listen to audio version of this article
Sponsored by
0:00 / 0:48
Instaread

According to a recent news release, on July 22, 2026 the Vermont State Police received a report of an illegal dumping of tires in the Willoughby State Forest in Sutton, Vermont.   Troopers responded to the area and located 40 tires on the edge of the road.  A vehicle of interest was seen in the area just prior to the tires being dumped on the side of the road.  A photograph of the vehicle is attached to this press release.

Anyone with information related to this incident or the possible identities of individuals involved is encouraged to contact Trooper Benjamin Goodwin at the St. Johnsbury Barracks at 802-748-3111. Anonymous tips may be provided online at https://vsp.vermont.gov/tipsubmit.
