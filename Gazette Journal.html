THURSDAY LEADERSHIP SEMINAR: The Gloucester Branch of the NAACP will have a Pathways to Leadership seminar on the topic of “Understand Your Board of Supervisors” at 6 o’clock this evening at Shepherdsville Baptist Church, 8729 Farys Mill Road, Gloucester. FRIDAY COMMUNITY SOCIAL: A Quarterly Veteran and First Responder Caregiver Community Social will be held from 10:30 a.m. to noon on Friday, July 24, at Coastal Church, 6686 Hickory Fork Road, Gloucester. It is free and open to the public. No RSVP required. HATCHERY TOURS: Oyster seed hatchery tours and tastings are offered from 5-9 p.m. on Friday, July 24, at Oyster Seed Holdings, 425 Callis Wharf Road, Grimstead. Tickets are $25, available at oshoyster.com/tours. SATURDAY OPEN HOUSES: The Fairfield Foundation will have open houses at Timberneck House at 3601 Timberneck Farm Road, Hayes and Walter Reed Birthplace at 4021 Hickory Fork Road, Gloucester, from 10 a.m. to 2 p.m. on Saturday, July 25.
