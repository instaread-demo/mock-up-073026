Christian Jensen Jr.

byThe New Era
July 27, 2026

July 30, 1943 – July 20, 2026

Christian Jensen Jr., 82, of Crawfordsville, passed away July 20, 2026.

He was born July 30, 1943, to Agnes (Hansen) and Christian Jensen, Sr. in Crawfordsville.

He lived in Crawfordsville his whole life. Christian worked as a millwright and superintendent, building and installing machinery all over the United States. He retired from O and S Construction.

He loved to restore and fix up classic cars and tractors. He raced at Willamette Speedway when he was younger.

Christian is survived by his wife, Phyllis Jensen of Crawfordsville; his daughter, Linda Anderson of Lebanon, his son, David Jensen of Crawfordsville,; four grandchildren and eight great-grandchildren. 

He was preceded in death by his siblings, Mary, Verna and Roy.

Graveside service will be at 10 a.m. Wednesday, July 29, at Union Crawfordsville Cemetery. Sweet Home Funeral Chapel is handling arrangements. www.sweethomefuneral.com.
