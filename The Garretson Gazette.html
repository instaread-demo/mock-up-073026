5th Annual Pack the Park Saturday Aug 1

Date:

July 23, 2026
Listen to audio version of this article
Sponsored by
0:00 / 1:48
Instaread

Over the past four years, the Garretson community has worked to honor the life of Benton Howe with Pack the Park, a fundraiser that benefits students in the community. 

Next Saturday, August 1, starting at 6 p.m. and going until late, the fifth annual Pack the Park event will take place at ﻿the Jaycee's Complex.

In its first year, Pack the Park accomplished many things, first and foremost by holding a community gathering in memory of 11-year-old Benton Howe, who lost his life in a Spring 2022 UTV accident. Baseball was one of his favorite sports to play. It raised thousands of dollars for the Benton Howe Memorial Scholarship, an endowment within the Garretson Community Scholarship Foundation that awards scholarships to college-bound Garretson High School graduates.

Now on its fifth year, Pack the Park showcases the community's youth baseball and softball players at the age between coach-pitch/kid pitch and the high school leagues. A round-robin wiffle ball game allows the 12U kids to get out on the field and have fun. During the evening, all youth tee-ball, softball, and baseball participants are invited to join the Player Parade, allowing the community to see the strength of the Garretson Ball programs.

Advertisement

This year, more activities have been added, including a beanbag tournament, a dunk tank, Mr. Twister balloon artist, and face painting, to go along with the ever-popular raffle and guess-the-number prize board. Other fun activities are likely to pop up during the evening!

Several food options will be available, including ice cream, energy drinks, and concessions. 

A few tailgate spots and raffle board tickets may still be available, reach out to Pack the Park organizers Cory Buchholz or Betsy Howe to purchase. Otherwise, plan to head to the park on Saturday, August 1 for some fun!
