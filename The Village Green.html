4 Candidates File to Run for 3 Seats on South Orange-Maplewood Board of Education

by The Village Green July 27, 2026
The Village Green July 27, 2026
0 FacebookTwitterPinterestEmail

Listen to audio version of this article
Sponsored by

As of the filing deadline on July 27, four candidates have filed to run for three seats on the South Orange-Maplewood Board of Education: Patricia Azeez, Liz Callahan, Kevin Diaz, and Will Meyer.

Callahan and Meyer — both of whom are incumbents — announced their candidacies earlier Monday.

Per her Facebook profile, Azeez is a graduate of Lafayette College and is the former owner at Premier Martial Arts Maplewood. She is a South Orange resident.

Diaz, a district parent who works as a real estate broker with Keller Williams. provided a press release, posted here.

Incumbent Shayna Sackett-Gable announced that she would not seek reelection.
