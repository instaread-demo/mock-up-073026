    Minor Heat Advisory is in effect for Davidson County until 8 PM CDT Tuesday, with heat index values expected to reach up to 110°F.

    Current conditions in Davidson County show a temperature of 87.8°F with a light wind from the southeast at 3.6 mph. There is no precipitation reported, and the sky remains clear.

    Earlier today, the high temperature reached 90.9°F, while the low was 71.2°F. Wind speeds varied, reaching up to 9.3 mph throughout the day, with a precipitation chance of 62%, resulting in a total of 0.08 in of slight rain during the day. Tonight, temperatures are expected to drop to a low of 80.6°F, with wind speeds moving to a lighter 4.7 mph and only a 1% chance of precipitation.

    Residents are advised to stay hydrated and take precautions against heat-related illnesses due to the ongoing heat advisory and high humidity levels.
