The weather today will be partly sunny, with highs in the upper 70s. West winds of around 5 to 10 Mph.

Ready to hit the dirt? Grab your helmet and meet DCAB at The Dip this Thursday, July 30 at 5:15 PM for a fun, free, non-competitive women’s trail ride. All abilities are welcome to learn new skills, snap pics of wildflowers, and make awesome biking friends.

Learn the basics of foraging on a ranger-led hike at Deep Creek Lake State Park on August 7th. You’ll learn how to identify local plants and mushrooms in a fun, hands-on environment. Meet at the Cherry Creek Parking Lot for a 1:00 PM start.

Garrett Regional Medical Center and the Garrett County Health Department are offering Walk With Ease, a free program running Mondays, Wednesdays, and Fridays from 12:00 PM to 1:00 PM at Broadford Park through August 14. Designed to reduce arthritis pain and increase overall activity in a supportive environment, you can sign up or learn more by calling 301-533-4554.

Catch Eric Lewis & Co. live tonight at the Honi-Honi Bar starting at 6:00 PM. Stop by to enjoy a great evening of live music and local entertainment!
